How the ballpark is laid out

Wrigley Field opened in 1914 and seats 41,649. It has six seating tiers: the Club Box level closest to the field, the Field Box 100s, the Terrace 200s, the Upper Box 300s and Upper Reserved 400s in the Southwest Airlines Deck, and the Budweiser Bleachers in the outfield.

Section numbers increase from the left-field foul pole, around behind home plate, toward the first-base and right-field side. Low numbers are the third-base and left-field side; high numbers are the first-base and right-field side. This is the opposite of the direction used at several other parks, so do not carry a habit over.

The sections directly behind home plate are:

  • Club Box Level (3-32): sections 13–22.
  • Field Box Level (100s): sections 112–122.
  • Terrace Level (200s): sections 213–222.
  • Upper Box (300s): sections 315–318.
  • Upper Reserved (400s): sections 415–419.

How the tiers stack

The home-plate block sits at different numbers on every tier — 13–22 on the Club Box level, 112–122 in the Field Box, 213–222 on the Terrace, 315–318 in the Upper Box. Adding 100 to a section number does not put you above the same spot.

Landmarks

  • Cubs (home) dugout: third-base side, the low-numbered side. Club Box sections 9–12 sit behind it.
  • Visiting dugout: first-base side, fronted by Club Box sections 23–27.
  • Both bullpens are under the bleachers, off the field of play, moved there before the 2017 season. The Cubs bullpen is under the left-field bleachers, the visiting bullpen under the right-field bleachers, both with windows.
  • Support posts are the signature obstruction here. Wrigley is a 1914 steel-post ballpark. On the Terrace 200 level, rows 1–6 are the only pole-free rows; from about row 7 back, posts are in play. Check the per-section notes below.
  • Compass: a batter at home plate faces roughly east-northeast. The left-field wall runs along Waveland Avenue to the north, right field along Sheffield to the east.

How seat numbers work at this park

Every section page for this ballpark states the same rule: facing the field, seat 1 is on your left. Combined with the direction the section numbers run, that resolves to one rule for the whole park:

Seat 1 sits on the edge of the section facing the next LOWER section number, and seat numbers count up toward the next HIGHER section number.

Where seat 1 sits, by half of the ballpark
Where you areSeat 1 is… Higher seat numbers are…
The first base / right field half (sections numbered above the home-plate block)closest to home plate farther from home plate

So a single rule of thumb will not cover the whole park. Each section entry below spells out which end of its row seat 1 is on.

Rows

Rows are numbers at Wrigley, not letters. Club Box runs rows 1–15; Field Box 1–15; Terrace rows 1–6 are Terrace Box and rows 7 and beyond are Terrace Reserved; Upper Box roughly 1–12; Upper Reserved roughly 1–9.

Accessibility landmarks

The official Cubs accessibility guide does not publish a list of accessible section numbers. It places accessible seating by proximity to an elevator, lift or ramp, and allows a guest with a mobility disability up to three companions.

  • Marquee Gate elevator — Terrace Level and Southwest Airlines Deck
  • Left Field Gate elevator — Terrace Level and Southwest Airlines Deck
  • Wintrust Right Field Gate elevator — Terrace Level and Southwest Airlines Deck
  • Left-field corner elevator — Field Box and the upper level of the Budweiser Bleachers
  • Right-field corner elevator — Field Box
  • Budweiser Bleacher Gate elevator — upper level of the Budweiser Bleachers

A complete section-and-row inventory of accessible seating was not available in text form when this was compiled, so the list above is a set of landmarks rather than a complete map. The ticket office can confirm specific seats.

Sources, confidence and caveats

Rows, entrance rows, zones and seat-numbering direction come from RateYourSeats section pages, one page fetched per section, with A View From My Seat as a fallback. Orientation, dugouts, bullpens, capacity and accessibility come from the team's own ballpark and disability access guides, cross-checked against independent ballpark guides. Every row of the section CSV carries its own confidence rating. Of 194 sections, 140 are high confidence, 21 medium and 33 low.

Known gaps

  • The seat-1 side is not settled. RateYourSeats contradicts itself: its individual section pages say seat 1 is on the left facing the field, while its seating chart overview page says seat 1 is on the far right. This page follows the section pages, which is the more specific source, but the whole seat-1 rule above rests on it. Treat it as unverified and check your ticket.
  • Bleacher sections 536, 537 and 538 are grouped with the Bleachers by one source and labelled Upper Reserved by another. Their real location is not confirmed.
  • The left-field versus right-field split of bleacher sections 501–515 is inferred from the numbering direction, not stated directly.
  • Sections 516, 517 and 518 have no individual section page; the URLs resolve to a zone page, so no row data exists for them.
  • Seats per row is not published for any section at this park.
