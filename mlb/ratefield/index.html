Rate Field: an accessible guide to every section

In order to assist in finding tickets for White Sox games, the following is an overview of the stadium, sections, and details about the location of each section in the stadium.

A seat map answers one question at a glance: where would I actually be sitting? This page answers the same question in words — for all 133 sections at Rate Field. What the section number means, where it sits relative to home plate, how deep it is, where the entrance is, and which end of the row seat 1 is on.

How the ballpark is laid out

Rate Field — Guaranteed Rate Field until December 2024, and Comiskey Park before that — opened in 1991 and seats 40,615. Three ticketed tiers: the Lower Level 100s, the Club Level 300s and the Upper Level 500s.

Section numbers increase from right field, up the first-base side, past home plate, down the third-base side and out to left field. Low numbers are the first-base and right-field side; high numbers are the third-base and left-field side.

The sections directly behind home plate are:

  • Lower Level (100s): sections 130–134.
  • Club Level (300s): sections 328–336.
  • Upper Level (500s): sections 529–535.

How the tiers stack

The home-plate block sits at 130–134 on the Lower Level, 328–336 on the Club Level and 529–535 on the Upper Level. There are no 200 or 400 series here at all, so the tiers jump 100 to 300 to 500.

Landmarks

  • White Sox (home) dugout: third-base side, fronted by sections 137–142.
  • Visiting dugout: first-base side, fronted by sections 122–127.
  • Scout Seats are the premium block closest to the plate, labelled 130S, 131S, 133S and 134S — the S suffix matters when you read a ticket.
  • Sections 157 and 158 sit above the White Sox bullpen, and section 157 has a foul-pole obstruction on seat 1.
  • The upper deck is famously steep — it is one of the steepest in the majors. Per-section notes below record what the source states.

How seat numbers work at this park

Every section page for this ballpark states the same rule: facing the field, seat 1 is on your right. Combined with the direction the section numbers run, that resolves to one rule for the whole park:

Seat 1 sits on the edge of the section facing the next LOWER section number, and seat numbers count up toward the next HIGHER section number.

So a single rule of thumb will not cover the whole park. Each section entry below spells out which end of its row seat 1 is on.

Rows

Rows are numbers here, with two wrinkles. Many Lower Level sections end in a row labelled WCH, which is the wheelchair-accessible row, and some sections (112–119, 147–150, 152) begin with a row labelled AA ahead of row 1.

Accessibility landmarks

Accessible seating is spread across the tiers. The WCH row label makes it unusually legible from the row list on this park's sections.

  • A WCH row is stated on many Lower Level sections
  • Sections 100, 114, 115, 116 and 117 state no WCH row
  • Section 133 shows no WCH row on the seating source despite appearing in the club's own accessibility guidance — worth confirming with the ticket office

A complete section-and-row inventory of accessible seating was not available in text form when this was compiled, so the list above is a set of landmarks rather than a complete map. The ticket office can confirm specific seats.

Sources, confidence and caveats

Rows, entrance rows, zones and seat-numbering direction come from RateYourSeats section pages, one page fetched per section, with A View From My Seat as a fallback. Orientation, dugouts, bullpens, capacity and accessibility come from the team's own ballpark and disability access guides, cross-checked against independent ballpark guides. Every row of the section CSV carries its own confidence rating. Of 133 sections, 109 are high confidence, 20 medium and 4 low.

Known gaps

  • The 300 and 500 series full extents differ between sources: one guide lists 301–359 and 501–559 while the ticketing source lists only the subsets documented here.
  • Sections 330 and 334 have no stated entrance row or seat direction; their row range is only implied by a general Club Level line about five rows.
  • Sections 516 and 548 each state rows labelled 6–21 while also stating an entrance at row 1. Both are recorded exactly as published rather than reconciled.
  • No concert configuration is documented.
