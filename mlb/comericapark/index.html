How the ballpark is laid out

Comerica Park opened in 2000 and seats 41,083. Three tiers: the Lower Level 100s, a short Mezzanine in the 200s and the Upper Level 300s. There is no section 335.

Section numbers increase from right field, up the first-base side, past home plate, down the third-base side and out to left field. Low numbers are the first-base and right-field side; high numbers are the third-base and left-field side.

The sections directly behind home plate are:

  • Lower Level (100s): sections 127–128.
  • Upper Level (300s): sections 327–328.

How the tiers stack

The home-plate block sits at 127–128 on the Lower Level and 327–328 up top. The Mezzanine is the odd one out — sections 210–219 run only along the right-field and first-base side and never reach behind the plate, so distances on that tier are left blank rather than guessed.

Landmarks

  • The Tigers dugout is on the THIRD-base side, fronted by sections 131–136. The visiting dugout is on first base, at 120–124.
  • Both bullpens sit behind the left-field wall — the Tigers at 147–148 and the visitors at 149–150.
  • Netting runs in front of sections 116–140, and the official statement describes a safety net running foul pole to foul pole.
  • Section 328 has a stated obstruction — the source describes it as obstructed by railings and plexiglass.

How seat numbers work at this park

Every section page for this ballpark states the same rule: facing the field, seat 1 is on your right. Combined with the direction the section numbers run, that resolves to one rule for the whole park:

Seat 1 sits on the edge of the section facing the next LOWER section number, and seat numbers count up toward the next HIGHER section number.

So a single rule of thumb will not cover the whole park. Each section entry below spells out which end of its row seat 1 is on.

Rows

Rows are mixed here, and the ADA convention is unusually clear: any row label ending in AC is the accessible row — you will see 33AC, 44AC, DAC, TAC and HHAC. Premium and outfield blocks use letters (A to F, A to Z then AA to GG, and HHH to KKK), while the main bowl uses numbers.

Accessibility landmarks

The AC row suffix marks accessible rows and appears directly in the published row labels, which makes them locatable without a separate inventory.

  • Rows 33AC and 44AC in sections 112, 113 and 114
  • Row 33AC in section 121
  • Row HHAC in section 144; row TAC in sections 147 and 148
  • Rows DAC in sections 327, 333, 337 and 343

A complete section-and-row inventory of accessible seating was not available in text form when this was compiled, so the list above is a set of landmarks rather than a complete map. The ticket office can confirm specific seats.

Sources, confidence and caveats

Rows, entrance rows, zones and seat-numbering direction come from RateYourSeats section pages, one page fetched per section, with A View From My Seat as a fallback. Orientation, dugouts, bullpens, capacity and accessibility come from the team's own ballpark and disability access guides, cross-checked against independent ballpark guides. Every row of the section CSV carries its own confidence rating. Of 123 sections, 113 are high confidence, 4 medium and 6 low.

Known gaps

  • The compass bearing is reported as south by some sources and south-east by others.
  • The ticketing source states the Tigers dugout fronts sections 131–136 while the discovery pass recorded 131–135. Both are within a section of each other.
  • Seats per row is not published for most sections.
  • Entrance rows are not published for sections 121–140 at all.
