# The Card Place for Windows 1.0.0

The first Windows version of The Card Place: Hearts, Euchre, Spades, Cribbage and Sheephead against the computer, entirely offline, built for JAWS and NVDA first.

These are native ports, not the web games in a window. The rules, the computer players and every sentence the games say are the same as the web and iPhone versions, and the same kinds of test suites stand behind them.

## What's in it

- **Five games** with their rule options:
  - points to win in each game;
  - stick the dealer and going alone in Euchre;
  - 61 or 121 in Cribbage;
  - three to six players, leasters and doublers in Sheephead.

  Euchre, Cribbage and Sheephead have Easy, Normal and Hard computer players.
- **Your hand is one Tab stop.**
  - Left and Right move card by card, Home and End go to the ends, and 1 to 9 jump straight to a card.
  - Enter or Space plays a card, or chooses it for a pass, a throw or a bury.
  - A card you can't play stays and says why.
- **The same keys as the web games:**
  - H, T, L, S and the others read your hand, the trick, the last trick and the scores;
  - N continues or deals;
  - R repeats;
  - G goes to the log;
  - ? lists the keys.
- **Menus with Alt or F10**, never on the Tab ring, with every review command in the Review menu.
- **Focus follows the turn,** to your first playable card or to your bid. It never pulls you out of an open menu or dialog.
- **Choose what you hear** in Settings ▸ Screen Reader Announcements:
  - results of what you do;
  - hints;
  - other players' plays, bids and calls;
  - tricks and scores;
  - deals and messages to you;
  - your turn.

  Refusals, and anything you ask for, are always spoken. Everything is in the game log and the status bar either way: Ctrl+F9 reads the status bar again, and Ctrl+Shift+F9 moves to it.
- **The pace of the computer players:** Immediate, Brisk, Comfortable, Relaxed, or Wait for me. Continue is always there during a pause.
- **A table drawn for everyone:**
  - a green felt with the seats around it in play order;
  - large cards with the suit printed;
  - an amber focus ring;
  - a hand that fans to fit.

  Windows high contrast themes are followed, live, and Ctrl+Plus zooms to 200%.

## Known limits

- Playing against other people isn't in this version.
- A game in progress isn't kept when you close the app.
- There's no Report a Bug form yet. Crashes are written to `%APPDATA%\TheCardPlace\TheCardPlace.log`.
