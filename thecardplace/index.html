The Card Place

Five card games, free to play in a browser. Mouse, keyboard or screen reader — all three work properly. No sign up, no download, nothing to install.

Choose a game

  • Euchre

    4 players · on your own or with other people

    The Midwest and Ontario standard. Two partnerships, five tricks a hand, first to ten points — and the jack of the same color as trump quietly becomes a trump too.

    Learn to play Euchre Play Euchre

  • Hearts

    4 players · on your own or with other people

    Take no points if you can help it. Every heart is one and the queen of spades is thirteen, and the lowest score wins — which is the thing that catches people out.

    Learn to play Hearts Play Hearts

  • Spades

    4 players · on your own or with other people

    Two partnerships, and a promise made before a card is played: say how many tricks you will take, then take them. Spades are always trump, missing the contract costs the whole bid, and taking too many quietly fills a bag bin that costs a hundred.

    Learn to play Spades Play Spades

  • Cribbage

    2 players · on your own or with somebody else

    Pegging, fifteens, runs and the crib, scored to 121 on a board you can read as well as see. You count your own hand, the way you would at a table.

    Learn to play Cribbage Play Cribbage

  • Sheephead

    3 to 6 players · on your own or with other people

    The Wisconsin classic, also called Schafkopf. Queens and jacks are trump, the picker takes the blind, and the jack of diamonds is a secret partner nobody admits to.

Why these exist

Most card games online cannot be played without sight. These are built the other way round: the state of the game is text first, and the pictures sit on top of it.

  • Nothing is shown by sight alone. Every card, score and turn is also text.
  • Nothing is marked as an application region, so browse, focus and forms modes work as they normally do.
  • Turn your text size up and the layout follows, cards included.
  • Every game can be played with the keyboard alone.

Every game runs in your browser and sends nothing anywhere until you open a table with other people. From then on it sends your moves and the name you chose, and nothing else. There are no accounts, and a table is a five-letter code that stops existing when everybody leaves.
